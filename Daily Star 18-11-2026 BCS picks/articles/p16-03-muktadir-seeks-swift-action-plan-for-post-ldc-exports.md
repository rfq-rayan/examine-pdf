# Muktadir seeks swift action plan for post- LDC exports

_STAR BUSINESS REPORT — Star Business — PDF p16_

![Khandakar Abdul Muktadir](../images/p16_03.jpg)

*Khandakar Abdul Muktadir *

Commerce Minister Khandakar Abdul Muktadir yesterday called for the swift finalisation of an action plan to boost Bangladesh’s exports after the country graduates from the least developed country (LDC) category in November 2029.

He made the call at a meeting at the commerce ministry to finalise the draft Country Programme Document (CPD) under the World Trade Organization’s (WTO) Enhanced Integrated Framework (EIF) Phase Three project, according to a press statement.

The EIF is a multilateral partnership that helps LDCs use trade to promote economic growth, sustainable development and poverty reduction.

The government is preparing the CPD under a three-year project worth $3.5 million to address trade challenges after LDC graduation and improve the country’s competitiveness. Of the total funding, around $2.5 million has been proposed for seven priority areas.

One of the proposed initiatives is an artificial intelligence-based market intelligence system to monitor and forecast international market trends in real time.

Other priorities include improving the capacity of the food processing industry and expanding international markets for products with geographical indication (GI) certification.

The programme also aims to strengthen the National Trade Facilitation Committee, according to a presentation by Hafizur Rahman, former director general of the WTO Cell and additional secretary at the commerce ministry.

Participants also discussed developing an environmental, social and governance (ESG) compliance framework, formulating a carbon market policy and training government officials and private-sector representatives to improve the country’s capacity in international trade negotiations.

Muktadir said the proposed initiatives could help make Bangladesh’s trade and export sectors more competitive in the post-LDC graduation period.
