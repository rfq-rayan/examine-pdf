# ACC seeks Interpol red notices against Hasina, family

_STAFF CORRESPONDENT — Dhaka — PDF p3_

The Anti-Corruption Commission (ACC) has sought Interpol red notices against ousted prime minister Sheikh Hasina, her son Sajeeb Wazed Joy, daughter Saima Wazed Putul and niece Tulip Siddiq in connection with 24 corruption cases.

ACC Secretary Md Saidur Rahman Khan told reporters yesterday that applications for Interpol red notices were submitted through the home and foreign ministries to facilitate the return of the individuals concerned through legal procedures.

The commission has also frozen movable and immovable assets worth Tk 1,062 crore linked to the cases, including 60 katha of land in Purbachal, a house in Baridhara, nine upscale flats and 204.40 decimals of land.

It has additionally traced assets belonging to Joy in the United States and sought details from the relevant US authorities through a Mutual Legal Assistance Request (MLAR).

Saidur said 24 corruption cases had so far been filed against Hasina and her family members. Investigations into 17 cases are ongoing, while charge sheets have been submitted to courts in seven. Verdicts have also been delivered in six cases relating to plot allocations in Purbachal.

The commission has also imposed travel restrictions on 15 individuals linked to the cases.
