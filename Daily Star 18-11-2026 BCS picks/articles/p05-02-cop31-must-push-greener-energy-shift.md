# COP31 must push greener energy shift

**Says EU climate chief; Australian negotiator defends draft as a tool for debate**

_REUTERS — Antalya — International — PDF p5_

![](../images/p05_02.jpg)

COP31 climate talks must send the “strongest possible signal” on the need to shift to clean energy, the European Union’s climate chief said yesterday, in an address to delegates divided over whether the world needs to keep burning fossil fuels.

A draft deal issued by the presidency of the talks on Monday reflected the extent of disagreements between the nearly 200 countries represented at the talks and highlighted the challenge of agreeing a meaningful deal by the conference’s official close on Friday.

EU climate commissioner Wopke Hoekstra said the draft text distracted from the need for sustained reductions in greenhouse gas emissions, which are primarily caused by burning oil, gas and coal.

“It is hugely important that here at this COP, that is about implementation, we do not get distracted by other things. And to be frank, that is unfortunately what the draft cover decision is doing,” Hoekstra said.

“It is absolutely critical that COP31 sends the strongest possible signal on the clean energy transition, and more generally for immediate, deep, and sustained GHG emission reductions,” he said.

Australia’s Chris Bowen, who is leading negotiations in the Mediterranean city of Antalya under a split presidency arrangement with host Turkey, said Monday’s 18-page draft was crucial to the debating process and sought to reflect the range of views. The draft contained two sets of phrasing around fossil fuels.
