# Quad leaders flag ‘economic coercion’

**Oppose unilateral actions undermining peace**

_REUTERS — New Delhi — International — PDF p5_

Leaders of the four-nation Quad grouping strongly oppose any destabilising or unilateral actions that seek to change the status quo, including by force or coercion, which escalate tensions and undermine regional peace and stability, a joint statement said yesterday.

The leaders also expressed grave concerns about economic coercion, including through arbitrary export restrictions, the statement added after their meeting in Manila. The summit was convened shortly after US President Donald Trump arrived in Manila to attend the Asean-US Summit.

“Our countries are the four corners of a free, open, stable, and prosperous Indo-Pacific,” Trump said in remarks ahead of the meeting. “We have outstanding relationships ... on matters of regional security, economic security and diplomacy.”

Trump’s 3-day trip to Asia, which also includes the Asia- Pacific Economic Cooperation forum in Shenzhen, comes after his Republican Party’s defeat in midterm elections.
