# Nearly half of upazila-level doctor posts vacant: minister

_STAFF CORRESPONDENT — Dhaka — PDF p3_

At least 6,288 of the 13,036 sanctioned physician posts (nearly 48%) at upazila health complexes across Bangladesh are currently vacant, according to Health Minister Sardar Md Sakhawat Husain.

During yesterday’s question-answer session at the parliament, State Minister for Health MA Muhit answered lawmakers’ queries in a written statement on behalf of the health minister, who was absent.

He informed that there are currently 42,549 physician posts across government hospitals, upazila health complexes, union health centres and specialised hospitals. Of these, 11,336 are vacant. Also, 21,656 of the 57,306 class three posts, including those for healthcare workers, remain unfilled, he added.

The state minister informed that the government is providing vaccinations to children countrywide.

The target for measles vaccination was 1,80,15,064 children, but the number vaccinated reached 2,17,91,259, representing 121 percent of the target.

He informed that deaths from heart disease and heart attacks among people aged 25-30, particularly men, have increased at an alarming rate in recent times.
