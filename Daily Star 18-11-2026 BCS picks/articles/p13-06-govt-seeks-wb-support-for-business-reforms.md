# Govt seeks WB support for business reforms

_STAR BUSINESS REPORT — Star Business — PDF p13_

Commerce Minister Khandakar Abdul Muktadir yesterday asked the World Bank to support business reforms and export diversification, saying the government aims to cut the time for key business services from 50-55 days or more to 14 days.

He outlined the plans at a morning meeting with an eight-member World Bank delegation, led by Jean Pesme, division director for Bangladesh and Bhutan, at the Secretariat in Dhaka.

The government is also preparing a three-year integrated action plan ahead of graduation from least developed country (LDC) status.

Muktadir said trade licence, environmental and fire safety clearance, and import registration certificate (IRC) services are being digitalised through the National Single Window’s one-stop portal, “Invest Bangladesh”.

He said leather and leather goods, non-leather footwear, shipbuilding and recycling, and light engineering were being given special importance alongside garments.

A central effluent treatment plant is planned at the Savar Leather Industrial Estate.

Talks on economic partnership agreements with Japan and South Korea, and free trade agreements with the UAE, Malaysia, Singapore, and the EU, are progressing, the minister said.
