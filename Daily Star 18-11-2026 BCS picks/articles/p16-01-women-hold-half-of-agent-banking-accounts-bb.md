# Women hold half of agent banking accounts: BB

_STAR BUSINESS REPORT — Star Business — PDF p16_

Women now hold nearly half of all accounts opened through agent banking in Bangladesh, highlighting their growing participation in the formal financial system, according to a Bangladesh Bank report.

Of the 27.8 million accounts opened through agent banking by September this year, around 13.8 million belonged to female customers.

The number of accounts held by women increased by 1.91 percent during the July-September quarter, outpacing the growth in male-owned accounts, the central bank said in its latest quarterly report on agent banking.

Agent banking allows customers to access financial services through outlets run by agents, particularly in areas where conventional bank branches are limited.

The sector continued to expand during the quarter, with 30 banks operating more than 20,600 outlets across the country. The number of outlets and agents grew by 0.41 percent and 0.54 percent respectively from the previous quarter.

The total number of accounts also increased by nearly 2 percent, reflecting rising demand for the service. Rural customers held 85 percent of all accounts, underlining the importance of agent banking in bringing financial services to people outside urban centres.

Deposits in agent banking accounts rose by 2.31 percent to Tk 53,108 crore by the end of September. Loans disbursed through the channel grew faster, increasing by 7.93 percent to Tk 44,110 crore.

Currently, 23 banks offer loans through agent banking outlets, highlighting the channel’s growing role in extending credit to customers.

Meanwhile, inward remittances distributed through agent outlets increased by 2.44 percent during the quarter to Tk 2.20 lakh crore, reflecting the channel’s importance in delivering money to beneficiaries, particularly in rural areas.

The report also highlighted the growing participation of women as agent banking entrepreneurs.

By September, 28 banks had 2,244 outlets owned by women, operating through 1,882 female-owned agent points.

These outlets served around 2.5 million accounts, 83 percent of which belonged to rural customers.

They held deposits of Tk 4,822 crore, disbursed loans worth Tk 3,582 crore and distributed inward remittances totalling Tk 5,546 crore.

The central bank said a circular issued in May 2025 requiring banks to maintain an equal ratio of male- and female-owned outlets was expected to encourage more women to become agent banking entrepreneurs.
