# Iran strikes US military radar system in Bahrain

**Warns ships against using ‘fake American’ route through Hormuz**

Bahrain said yesterday it intercepted Iranian air attacks, after Iran’s Revolutionary Guards said they had targeted a US military radar system at Sheikh Isa Air Base in the kingdom with drones.

The attack was in response to what they described as continued violations of Iranian airspace by US military drones. The Guards had seized control of a US military drone south of Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz and brought it down intact later on Monday.

The reported attacks in Bahrain - which is home to the US Navy’s Fifth Fleet - come amid stalled diplomatic efforts to end the US-Israeli war on Iran, which began on February 28 and has spread across the region.

Earlier yesterday, Bahrain warned of an unspecified threat and urged citizens and residents to head to the nearest safe location, reports Reuters.

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards also warned vessels against taking “fake American routes” through the Hormuz as attacks on commercial vessels in the critical waterway continue.

Meanwhile, new US intelligence indicates that Iran is developing plans to target senior Trump administration officials by detonating a so-called dirty bomb or a chemical weapon in Washington, DC, even as officials continue to debate whether Iran can or will attempt to execute such a plot, according to seven US officials.

Sources familiar with the latest intelligence characterized the Iranian planning as, for now, an effort to give itself options rather than an indicator of an imminent attack, reports CNN.

But the plotting picked up by the US has been very specific and includes locations and targets, two of the sources said, and has sparked a flurry of US contingency planning.
